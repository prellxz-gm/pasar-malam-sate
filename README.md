**Pasar Malam: Sate** is a night-market satay grill game. You run a satay stall at dusk under string lights: grill skewers of chicken, beef, lamb, tofu and tempeh over glowing charcoal, flip each one, pull it off in the green PERFECT window, dip it in peanut, kecap or sambal sauce, add lontong or iced tea when the ticket asks, and serve it before the customer's patience runs out. Coals cool over time — stoke them with the fan, but a flare-up burns everything fast. Perfect serves build a combo up to x5; a 180-second shift ends with a rush hour in the middle.

## How to play

- Click a raw skewer (or Z X C V B) to put it on the grill.
- Click a skewer on the grill to flip it. When its doneness meter is in the green zone, click again to take it off.
- Click a sauce pot (A S D) to dip the skewer in your hand. Click the lontong basket (L) or the drinks cooler (T) for sides.
- Click a customer ticket to place what you hold and complete the order.
- Press F or click the fan to stoke the coals. M mutes, P pauses, R restarts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests, all art drawn in canvas, all sounds synthesized with WebAudio.
